Abstract

I work at the intersection of Bayesian methodology and applied uncertainty quantification. My core interest is in how distributions behave when the variance itself is uncertain — theoretically, in variance-mixture models, and practically, wherever a prediction needs to know what it doesn't know. I've applied this to regime-adaptive shrinkage for financial time series at IIT Kanpur, and to conformal prediction for fingerprint classification as a forensic pattern-evidence problem. I'm currently pursuing an M.S. in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at IIITM Gwalior (CGPA 9.24/10), following a B.Sc. in Statistics and Data Analytics at Adamas University (CGPA 9.18/10, Silver Medalist).
